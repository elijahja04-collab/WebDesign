# Assignment 4
## Visual Appeal
### I used a home icon, info icon, and about icon to go on my navigation links. It makes it even more clear where these lead to as the icons make sense with each page's purpose. I used my blue color across the website so the website feels clean and consistent and not a splurge of color. I used a clean white background and black text for most of the site, and used blue either to outline for an accent so it does not look so boring, or used it to draw attetnion to certian sections such as my blockquote component. I also used lines above and below my headers to make it very clear what they are and where they stop and the content starts. I used "prompt" and "oswald" as I think they complement each other well. Another reason I chose these is I want it to feel like a casual webiste, although not TOO casual and I think these capture that well. 
## Structure & Org
### My table organizes all of my gear so that my viewers can know all what I wear. I get asked this question a lot, so this could really be a good use case for the website. I chose to make my form match the rest of my site as far as the box, the background, and font styles of course. I made the submit button blue so it stands out and matches the rest of my site aswell. 
## Design Goals
### The goal of my form is to collect user information so that I can reach out to potential sponsors, or other influencers looking to connect. It also has a fun section so It doesn't feel like they are selling their information to me. The gallery shows off my bike, me on the bike, and me taking the bike to the track. Its a fun piece of the website. I used a 3x3 pattern because it seemed to give the most "clean" look to me.
